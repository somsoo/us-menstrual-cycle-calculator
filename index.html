🧮 Calculation Methodology

  • Standard Luteal Phase (14 Days): While the follicular phase before ovulation can vary, the luteal phase between ovulation and menstruation remains relatively constant at approximately 14 days in healthy menstrual cycles. Ovulation date is determined by subtracting 14 days from the next expected period start date.
  • The 6-Day Fertile Window: Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, while an unfertilized ovum remains viable for roughly 12 to 24 hours. The clinical fertile window therefore spans the 5 days preceding ovulation through 1 day following.
  • 100% In-Browser Privacy: With heightened concerns around health data surveillance, our tool processes all cycle timelines locally within your device's browser memory. No tracking cookies or cloud databases are used.

Clinical guidelines reference: American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG), Practice Bulletin No. 193: Menstrual Cycles in Adults.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the calculator predict ovulation and fertile windows?

The calculator uses clinical gynecological guidelines (ACOG standards). Because the luteal phase between ovulation and the next period consistently averages 14 days in healthy cycles, ovulation is calculated by subtracting 14 days from your next expected period date. The fertile window spans from 5 days prior to ovulation through 1 day after.

Is my reproductive and cycle data kept private?

Yes, 100%. Unlike commercial period tracking apps that monetize user health profiles, this calculator runs entirely on your device inside your web browser. No dates or cycle details are ever sent to an external server or stored in a remote database.

Can this calculator be used for natural contraception?

No. Due to natural physiological variations caused by stress, sleep, illness, and hormonal fluctuations, mathematical cycle estimation should not be relied upon as a primary contraceptive method.

What is considered a normal cycle length?

According to the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG), a normal adult menstrual cycle typically ranges between 21 and 35 days, with 28 days being the statistical average.
